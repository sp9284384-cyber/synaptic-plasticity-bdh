# AI Assistance & Provenance Disclosure

## What AI assisted with
- Drafting of initial code structure for `public/index.html` (canvas rendering, slider wiring,
  Hebbian update loop) based on the equations and design decisions specified by the team.
- Drafting of README/one-pager prose, subsequently reviewed and edited by the team.
- Locating and verifying primary-source quotations from the Dragon Hatchling paper via web search,
  cross-checked against the arXiv source (arXiv:2509.26507) before inclusion.

## What the team owns and can defend
- The choice of concept (Synaptic Plasticity as Short-Term Memory) and the one-sentence claim.
- The mapping between the toy Hebbian/decay equations and BDH's σ(i,j) formalism (data/bdh_equations.md).
- The interference design (why pattern B shares neuron 2 with pattern A, and what that is meant to
  demonstrate and NOT demonstrate — see docs/limitations.md).
- All citations in data/primary_papers.json — the team has read each paper's relevant section, not
  just the abstract.

## Forks / reused components
- No forked repository. `public/index.html` is written from scratch (vanilla HTML/CSS/JS, no
  external libraries, no build step) specifically for this submission.

## Assets
- No third-party images, fonts, or datasets were used. All visuals are generated by the canvas code
  at runtime.
